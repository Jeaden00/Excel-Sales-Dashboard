# Food & Beverage Sales Performance Dashboard

### Interactive Excel Dashboard and Executive-Level Business Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes Food & Beverage sales performance using Microsoft
Excel. An interactive dashboard was developed to monitor key business
performance indicators, evaluate sales trends, analyze product and sales
channel contributions, and assess manager, supervisor, and salesperson
performance.

The project also includes an Executive-Level Sales Performance Report
that translates analytical findings into business insights, management
priorities, and recommended actions.

## 2. Business Objectives

-   Monitor overall sales performance through key performance indicators
    (KPIs).
-   Analyze monthly sales trends and business momentum.
-   Evaluate product category and sales channel contributions.
-   Assess manager, supervisor, and salesperson performance.
-   Identify business risks and opportunities for revenue growth.
-   Provide data-driven recommendations to support management decisions.

## 4. Dashboard Features

-   **Sales Performance Monitoring:** Total Sales, Total Orders, and
    Average Order Value.
-   **Monthly Sales Trend:** Tracks sales performance and monthly
    fluctuations.
-   **Product Category Analysis:** Compares Food and Drinks sales
    contributions.
-   **Sales Channel Analysis:** Evaluates Retail, Distributor, and
    Online performance.
-   **Management Performance:** Analyzes sales contributions by Manager
    and Supervisor.
-   **Top Product Analysis:** Displays the 15 highest-performing
    products by sales.
-   **Salesperson Performance:** Compares individual salesperson
    contributions.
-   **Interactive Filters:** Allows users to explore results by month,
    product category, channel, manager, supervisor, and salesperson.

## 5. Key Business Insights

### Product Category Performance

-   Food contributes approximately 93% of total sales.
-   Drinks account for approximately 7%, representing an opportunity for
    category diversification.

### Sales Channel Performance

  Channel           Sales   Approx. Share
  ------------- --------- ---------------
  Retail          ₱10.01M             49%
  Distributor      ₱6.91M             34%
  Online           ₱3.45M             17%

### Product Performance

-   Standard White Flour leads product sales at approximately ₱1.06M.
-   Premium Bread Flour and Lollipop Twirls are also among the
    top-performing products.

### Sales Trend

Sales show positive long-term momentum, with strong performance around
September--October 2023, followed by a decline that warrants further
investigation.

### Average Order Value

The current AOV is ₱28,331.89. If the number of orders remained at 719
and AOV increased to ₱30,000, illustrative sales would be approximately
₱21.57M, or about ₱1.20M above current sales.

## 6. Business Recommendations

1.  Increase Average Order Value through product bundling,
    cross-selling, minimum-order incentives, and premium alternatives.
2.  Strengthen Online sales through targeted promotions,
    online-exclusive bundles, and repeat-purchase initiatives.
3.  Grow Drinks sales through Food and Drink combinations and beverage
    add-on offers.
4.  Improve sales team consistency by benchmarking high-performing teams
    and sharing effective practices.
5.  Protect top-selling products through inventory planning and
    availability monitoring.
6.  Investigate the decline following the September--October 2023 peak
    by reviewing product, channel, customer, and sales-team performance.
